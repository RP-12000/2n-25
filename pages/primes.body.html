Which n can work

Only odd n: for even n, 2n − 25 = (2n/2 − 5)(2n/2 + 5). And n ≡ 5 (mod 6) gives 2n ≡ 4 ≡ 25 (mod 7), so 7 divides it; n = 5 is the one exception, where 25 − 25 = 7 itself.

Prime or probable prime

Each entry from n = 33 up passed a base-3 Fermat test (PRP-3) in Prime95 with Gerbicz error checking. 2n − 25 has no large factored part in N − 1 or N + 1, so the large ones cannot be proven prime with today's methods; the small ones can be proven with ECPP.
