Sieve and factoring

Before any PRP test, every prime p up to the sieve depth is tried against all n at once: the siever solves 2n ≡ 25 (mod p) and strikes out each n that p divides. Removing a candidate this way costs a fraction of a second; a PRP test costs most of a core-hour.
